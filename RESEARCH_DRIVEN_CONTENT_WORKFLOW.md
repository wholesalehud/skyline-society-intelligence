# Research-Driven Content & Blog Strategy Workflow
## How the 6 Slash Commands Inform Your Content Decisions

---

## 🎯 **The Core Philosophy**

**RESEARCH FIRST → INSIGHTS → CONTENT STRATEGY → EXECUTION**

Never create content blindly. Every blog post, TikTok video, and Instagram post should be informed by **real market intelligence**.

---

## 📊 **The Research → Content Pipeline**

### **Phase 1: Gather Intelligence** (Monday - Research Day)

Run all research commands to gather data:

```bash
# Terminal commands (or use Claude Code UI)
claude /research-raleigh-market
claude /analyze-tiktok-performance
claude /mine-monetization-ideas
claude /discover-local-partners
```

**What you'll learn**:
- What people in Raleigh are searching for (SEO keywords)
- What questions they're asking on Reddit (blog topics)
- What TikTok content is performing best (content format)
- What partnerships could amplify reach (collaboration opportunities)

---

### **Phase 3: Prioritize Content** (Wednesday - Planning Day)

Use `/forecast-revenue-scenarios` to prioritize:

**Content that Drives Revenue** (High Priority):
1. SEO blog posts that rank for "raleigh proposal venue" (brings booking inquiries)
2. TikTok content that gets saves (high purchase intent)
3. Partnership content that generates referrals

**Content that Builds Awareness** (Medium Priority):
1. Viral TikTok formats (follower growth)
2. Instagram Reels (algorithm loves them)
3. Educational blog posts (establishes authority)

**Content that Fills Gaps** (Low Priority):
1. Random aesthetic posts (when you have time)
2. Personal brand content (nice to have)

---

### **Phase 4: Create Content** (Thursday-Friday - Production Days)

**Blog Writing Workflow**:

1. **Pick topic from research** (e.g., "Best Raleigh Proposal Venues 2025")

2. **Use research data to write**:
   - From `/research-raleigh-market`: List of competitor venues, pricing, features
   - Make Skyline Society #1 or #2 in the listicle (strategic placement)
   - Include real data: "Prices range from $150-400/hour in downtown Raleigh"

3. **SEO optimize**:
   - Primary keyword: "raleigh proposal venue"
   - Secondary keywords: From Google Trends data in research
   - Internal links: Link to booking page, pricing page

4. **Add CTAs based on monetization research**:
   - "Book your proposal consultation" (main CTA)
   - "Download our free Raleigh Proposal Planning Checklist" (email capture)
   - Affiliate links to recommended partners

**TikTok Creation Workflow**:

1. **Use winning format from `/analyze-tiktok-performance`**
   - Your winner: Livestream flower prep
   - Format: [Hook] + [Process] + [Reveal] + [CTA]

2. **Hook from market research**:
   - "Raleigh brides, you need to see this..."
   - "Downtown Raleigh's secret proposal spot is..."

3. **Content informed by Reddit discussions**:
   - Saw people complaining about parking? → Address it
   - "Here's the best parking for downtown Raleigh proposals..."

4. **CTA based on monetization strategy**:
   - If selling digital product: "Link in bio for full guide"
   - If building partnerships: "Tag your favorite Raleigh florist"
   - If growing email: "DM me 'RALEIGH' for proposal checklist"

## 💡 **How to Use `/generate-innovation-concepts` for Content**

When you run this command, it generates experimental ideas. **Test them through content first**:

**Innovation Concept**: "AR proposal previews"
↓
**Content Test**: Create TikTok about "What if you could see your proposal setup before booking?"
↓
**Measure Engagement**: High comments/saves = validate the concept
↓
**Decision**: If validated, build the AR feature. If not, move on.

**Innovation Concept**: "Content creator residency"
↓
**Content Test**: Blog post "I'm giving away free venue access to 5 Raleigh creators"
↓
**Measure Interest**: Applications received = validate demand
↓
**Decision**: Launch the program if interest is high.

## 🎯 **Success Metrics to Track**

### **Research Metrics** (Are we learning?)
- Keywords discovered per research session
- Competitor insights gathered
- Partner leads identified
- Monetization ideas validated

### **Content Metrics** (Is it working?)
- Blog traffic from organic search
- TikTok saves (high intent signal)
- Instagram engagement rate
- Email signups from content
- **Bookings attributed to content** (THE KEY METRIC)

### **Revenue Metrics** (Is it profitable?)
- Cost per blog post: $[time] → Traffic → Bookings → Revenue
- ROI per TikTok: $0 (just time) → Followers → Inquiries → Bookings
- Partnership content: $0 → Cross-promotion → Referrals → Revenue
