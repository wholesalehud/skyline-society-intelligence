### **Q: Do I need to run all 6 commands every week?**
A: No. Weekly: run 2-3 research commands. Monthly: run revenue forecasting and innovation concepts.

### **Q: What if I don't have TikTok analytics yet?**
A: `/analyze-tiktok-performance` will guide you through setting up TikTok Business account to get analytics.

### **Q: Can I customize the commands?**
A: Yes! Edit the `.md` files in `.claude/commands/` to fit your specific needs.

### **Q: How do I share these findings with my sister?**
A: Each command outputs a structured report. Copy-paste the relevant sections or export to PDF.

### **Q: What's the time commitment?**
A: Week 1: 10-15 hours. Ongoing: 5-10 hours/week. Decreases as automation kicks in.
